OpenWRT x86 r43166 Highmem SMP

October 30, 2014 PowerRC
OpenWRT

This time , I compiled with a stable version of Openwrt (Barrier Breaker) , instead of develop trunk version .

Similar to last time:

1.Supports memory over 4G . 2.Supports SMP , mulit-cores , HyperThreading up to 8 logical cores. 3.All default options of compiling , and I turn on options of USB-HID ( to support usb keyboard) 4.I compiled all packages based on my kernel , you could install them with setting of local repositories. Please refer to offcial wiki about this part =>http://wiki.openwrt.org/doc/techref/opkg#local.repositories 5.With virtual box image file ( only sda version)

If you have questions , please take a look at this FAQ first=》https://www.powerrc.net/openwrt-faq-en.html
